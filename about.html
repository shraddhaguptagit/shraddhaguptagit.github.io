Hi, I'm Shraddha.

I'm a writer and cultural studies student based in Bangalore & Bhopal. My work explores gender, culture, and the politics of everyday life, usually through a mix of research and personal reflection.

I like starting small — a feeling, a passing thought, a conversation — and following it until it becomes something clearer. Most of my writing sits somewhere between thinking and feeling, trying to make sense of both.

Alongside writing, I also like to doodle and create illustrations inspired by things I observe around me. It's mostly a hobby, but it helps me pay attention differently and hold onto details I might otherwise miss.
